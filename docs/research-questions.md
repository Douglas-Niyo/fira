# Research questions (draft v0.1, October 2026)

**RQ1 (main).** In small-entity accounts payable and vendor data, does combining
transparent rules with simple unsupervised scoring rank injected irregularities higher
than rules alone, at a fixed weekly review capacity (precision@k, recall at fixed workload)?

**RQ2.** Which automated checks can credibly serve as compensating controls when one
person holds incompatible duties, and how do they map to the GAO Green Book and COSO principles?

**RQ3.** Do plain-language explanations make reviewers faster and more accurate?

**RQ4 (later).** Can the method move between ERP systems with only a field-mapping change?

The evaluation plan will be published here *before* any results are run.
